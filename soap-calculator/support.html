FAQ

Are the recipe results safe to follow?

Lye is caustic and can cause severe chemical burns. Soap Calculator's results, including lye amounts, are estimates. Verify every recipe with a second calculator before you make a batch. The developer is not liable for batches or injuries. The app is not professional soapmaking or safety advice. See the Terms of Use.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Soap Calculator and use Restore Purchases in the app. Restoring asks Apple to re-check your Apple ID for an active Soap Calculator Premium subscription. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Where are my recipes stored?

On your iPhone. Soap Calculator runs fully on your device. There is no account, and recipe calculations are not sent to a server run by the developer. If iCloud or another device backup is on, Apple may include on-device app data in the backup under your backup settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings. On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apple ID (your name) > Subscriptions, select Soap Calculator Premium, and cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. You keep Premium until the period you've already paid for ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What are the Premium plans?

Premium Monthly is $8.99/month and includes a 1-week free introductory offer for eligible new users. Premium Yearly is $24.99/year with no trial. Prices are U.S.; other regions may differ. Subscriptions are handled by Apple and auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. See the Terms of Use for full details.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The app collects no data. Recipe calculations stay on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for the full story.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes recipes and settings stored on the device. If iCloud or another device backup is on, copies may remain in that backup until you delete them under your backup settings. Cancel your subscription separately in Apple ID settings if you no longer want to be billed.
