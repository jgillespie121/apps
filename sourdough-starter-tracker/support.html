FAQ

How do I restore purchases?

Open Sourdough Starter Tracker and use Restore Purchases on the Premium / paywall screen (or in Settings inside the app). Restoring asks Apple to re-check your Apple ID for an active subscription for Starter (Sourdough Starter Tracker). Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy Premium.

Why aren't feeding reminders firing?

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Check that notifications are allowed for Starter (Sourdough Starter Tracker) in Settings > Notifications. If permission is off, the app can't show a reminder. Focus and Do Not Disturb can also silence notifications that are otherwise allowed.

Why isn't the widget updating?

The home screen widget reads the app's data on your device through an App Group. Open Sourdough Starter Tracker so it can refresh that on-device data, then check the widget again. iOS also chooses when widgets refresh. If it still looks wrong, remove the widget and add it again from the home screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apple ID (your name) > Subscriptions, select Starter (Sourdough Starter Tracker), and cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. You keep Premium until the period you've already paid for ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What are the Premium plans?

Premium Monthly is $8.99/month and includes a 1-week free introductory offer for eligible new subscribers. Premium Yearly is $24.99/year. Prices are U.S.; other regions may differ. Subscriptions are handled by Apple and auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. See the Terms of Use for full details.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Starter logs, feeding history, notes, and optional photos stay on your phone. Nothing is sent to any server by me. See the Privacy Policy for the full story.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes starter logs, feeding history, notes, optional photos, and settings stored on the device, including data the widget reads. If iCloud or another device backup is on, copies may remain in that backup until you delete them under your backup settings. Cancel your subscription separately in Apple ID settings if you no longer want to be billed.
