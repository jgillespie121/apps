Apps · Egg Tracker: Coopmark

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Coopmark (the "app," listed on the App Store as "Egg Tracker: Coopmark") is made by John Gillespie, an independent developer ("I," "me"). This policy explains, in plain English, what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect your personal data. Everything it uses stays on your iPhone.

The short version

  • Daily egg counts, the per-hen laying log, egg sales, feed costs, and app settings stay on your iPhone. They are stored on the device with SwiftData.
  • Optional photos you choose stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • The optional reminder is a local notification, scheduled on your iPhone only.
  • Trend charts and a CSV export, when you use them, stay on your device.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party software development kits (SDKs), and no data is collected by me.
  • Nothing is sent to any server by me.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. I never see your payment card details.
  • I do not sell your data. I don't have any to sell.

Information stored on your device

Your daily egg counts, per-hen laying log, egg sales, feed costs, optional photos, reminder, and app settings are stored locally on your iPhone with SwiftData. I don't operate any servers, and this information is not sent to me or to anyone else.

Optional photos are chosen by you and stored only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded.

Trend charts are drawn on your iPhone from the egg logs stored on the device.

If your iPhone's iCloud device backup (or another device backup) is turned on, Apple may include this on-device data in that backup, according to your backup settings. I don't receive that backup.

Egg reminder

The app can schedule an optional local notification to remind you to log eggs. That notification is scheduled on your iPhone only. It is not sent through a server I run. You can turn notifications on or off anytime in Settings > Notifications.

CSV export

The app can export your logs as a CSV file on your iPhone. That file is created on your device. I don't receive it. If you save it or share it with another app, that copy is handled by you and by that app.

Purchases

Coopmark Premium subscriptions are purchased and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment, and I never receive your payment card information. The app checks with Apple (using Apple's StoreKit) to know whether you have an active subscription. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

The app has no sign-up or login. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party software development kits (SDKs), and no third-party tracking. It does not track you across other apps or websites. Nothing in the app is sent to any server by me. I do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal data, there is nothing to collect from a child either. If you have questions, contact me using the details below.

Your choices and deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your iPhone, you're in control:

  • Turn off notifications anytime in Settings > Notifications.
  • Delete your data by deleting the app. Removing Coopmark from your iPhone deletes your egg counts, per-hen laying log, egg sales, feed costs, optional photos, reminder, and settings stored on the device. (If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, copies may remain in those backups until they're replaced or deleted under your backup settings.)
  • Cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

California residents

Coopmark does not collect, sell, or share personal information as those terms are used in California privacy law. If you have a question about your privacy rights, contact me below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, such as the app starting to collect new information, I'll update this policy before the change takes effect.
