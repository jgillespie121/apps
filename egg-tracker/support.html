FAQ

How do I restore purchases?

Open Coopmark and use Restore Purchases in the app. Restoring asks Apple to re-check your Apple ID for an active subscription for Coopmark (Egg Tracker: Coopmark). Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Where are my egg logs stored?

On your iPhone. Coopmark stores daily egg counts, the per-hen laying log, egg sales, feed costs, optional photos, and settings on the device with SwiftData. There is no account, and nothing is sent to a server run by the developer. Optional photos you choose stay on the device and are never uploaded. If iCloud or another device backup is on, Apple may include that on-device data in the backup under your backup settings.

Why isn't the reminder firing?

The reminder is an optional local notification scheduled on your iPhone. Check that notifications are allowed for Coopmark (Egg Tracker: Coopmark) in Settings > Notifications. If permission is off, the app can't show a reminder. Focus and Do Not Disturb can also silence notifications that are otherwise allowed.

Where does the CSV export go?

Coopmark creates the CSV file on your iPhone from the logs stored on the device. The developer does not receive the file. If you save or share the CSV, that copy is handled by you and by the app you share it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings. On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apple ID (your name) > Subscriptions, select Coopmark (Egg Tracker: Coopmark), and cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. You keep your subscription until the period you've already paid for ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What are the subscription plans?

Coopmark Premium Monthly is $8.99/month and includes a 1-week free introductory offer for eligible new subscribers. Coopmark Premium Yearly is $24.99/year with no free trial. Prices are U.S.; other regions may differ. Subscriptions are handled by Apple and auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. See the Terms of Use for full details.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Egg counts, the per-hen laying log, sales, feed costs, and optional photos stay on your phone. No data is collected by the developer. See the Privacy Policy for the full story.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes egg counts, the per-hen laying log, egg sales, feed costs, optional photos, the reminder, and settings stored on the device. If iCloud or another device backup is on, copies may remain in that backup until you delete them under your backup settings. Cancel your subscription separately in Apple ID settings if you no longer want to be billed.
