2. The app, free use, and Premium

PostLine is a fence calculator on your iPhone. It estimates posts, rails, and pickets for a wood privacy, picket, chain-link, or vinyl fence, in feet or meters, and can include a gate.

Premium is an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited projects with multiple sections, corners, and gates; concrete bags and gravel; a cost estimate with editable prices and a waste percentage; a shopping list and a PDF materials list; and stain or paint coverage.
