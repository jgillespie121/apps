Apps · Fence Calculator: PostLine

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

PostLine (the "app," listed on the App Store as "Fence Calculator: PostLine") is made by John Gillespie, an independent developer ("I," "me"). This policy explains, in plain English, what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect your personal data. Everything it uses stays on your iPhone.

The short version

  • Your projects, measurements, photos, helper conversations and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • When you use the project helper or product search, what you send (your message, a short project summary, and any photo or camera frame you choose to share) goes to PostLine's server and to Anthropic to produce the answer. It is not stored after the answer is sent.
  • The server keeps only usage counts (how many helper messages and searches were used), tied to a random install ID or your App Store purchase ID. No name, email, location or contact details.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. I never see your payment card details.
  • I do not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

Your projects (fences, raised beds, chicken coops and anything else you plan), measurements, material choices, saved prices, shopping lists, helper conversations, photos you add to a conversation, and app settings are stored locally on your iPhone. Calculations are done on your iPhone.

If your iPhone's iCloud device backup (or another device backup) is turned on, Apple may include this data in that backup, according to your backup settings. I don't receive that backup.

The project helper and product search

The helper and product search only run when you use them. When you send a message, PostLine sends that message, the recent conversation for that project, a short text summary of the project (for example its size and materials), and any photo you attached to PostLine's server (hosted by Supabase in the United States). The server passes it to Anthropic's Claude models to write the answer, and for product search, to look up products on Amazon, Home Depot and Lowe's websites. In live help, what you say is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's speech recognition, and that text plus a still frame from your camera is sent the same way each time you stop talking. Audio and video are not sent or recorded.

PostLine's server does not store your messages, photos or camera frames. Anthropic processes them to produce the answer under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

To enforce usage limits and prevent abuse, the server keeps a count of helper messages and product searches. For the free plan the count is tied to a random ID created on your iPhone; for paid plans it's tied to the original transaction ID of your App Store purchase, so your allowance follows your purchase. These counts contain nothing else about you.

Shopping links

Order buttons open Amazon, Home Depot or Lowe's in your browser or their app. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies. As an Amazon Associate, PostLine earns from qualifying purchases made through its Amazon links; the link includes PostLine's associate tag but no information about you.

Camera, microphone and photos

PostLine asks for the camera when you take a project photo or start live help, and for the microphone and speech recognition only for live help. Photos you choose are saved inside PostLine's own storage on your iPhone. You can turn these permissions off any time in Settings.

Purchases

PostLine Lifetime (a one-time purchase) and PostLine Max (a monthly subscription) are bought and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment, and I never receive your payment card information. The app checks with Apple (using Apple's StoreKit) which purchases you have, and sends Apple's signed purchase record to PostLine's server so it can apply your plan's allowance. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

The app has no sign-up or login. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete a project in the app to remove it, its conversation and its photos from your iPhone.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app. (Copies may remain in device backups until they're replaced or deleted under your backup settings.)
  • Usage counts on the server contain no personal details; email me if you'd like the counts for your install or purchase removed.

California residents

PostLine does not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in California privacy law. If you have a question about your privacy rights, contact me below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, such as the app starting to collect new information, I'll update this policy before the change takes effect.
