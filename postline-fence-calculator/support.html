FAQ

How do I restore purchases?

Open PostLine and use Restore Purchases in the app. Restoring asks Apple to re-check your Apple ID for your PostLine Lifetime or Max purchase (Fence Calculator: PostLine). Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Where are my fence projects stored?

On your iPhone. PostLine stores projects, measurements, gates, and settings on the device with SwiftData. A project can be a wood privacy, picket, chain-link, or vinyl fence, in feet or meters. There is no account, and nothing is sent to a server run by the developer. If iCloud or another device backup is on, Apple may include that on-device data in the backup under your backup settings.

What is free, and what do Lifetime and Max include?

Free includes one project (a fence, raised bed, chicken coop or anything else) with every feature unlocked until you mark it complete, plus 15 helper messages and 2 product searches. PostLine Lifetime ($9.99 once) unlocks unlimited projects, every calculator and ordering, with 60 helper messages and 10 product searches a month. PostLine Max ($24.99 a month) adds photo measuring marks and live camera help, with 600 helper messages and 60 product searches a month.

Where does the shopping list or PDF go?

The shopping list and PDF materials list are free on your first project and part of Lifetime after that. PostLine creates them on your iPhone from the project stored on the device. The developer does not receive the file. If you save or share the PDF, that copy is handled by you and by the app you share it with.

Is anything a subscription? How do I cancel Max?

PostLine Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews. PostLine Max is a monthly subscription that renews until you cancel; cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or from Settings in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel Max. See the Terms of Use.

What does the project helper send, and is it stored?

Only when you use it: your message, a short summary of the project and any photo or camera frame you share go to PostLine's server and Anthropic to write the answer. They are not stored. See the Privacy Policy.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Fence projects and measurements stay on your phone. No data is collected by the developer. See the Privacy Policy for the full story.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes fence projects, measurements, saved prices, waste percentage, and settings stored on the device. If iCloud or another device backup is on, copies may remain in that backup until you delete them under your backup settings.
