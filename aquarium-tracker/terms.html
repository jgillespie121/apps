2. The app, free use, and Premium

Brineleaf keeps a water-test log and a tank-care schedule on your iPhone. You can record pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, and temperature, and optionally GH, KH, and salinity. The care schedule covers water changes, filter cleaning, and feeding, with reminders on your iPhone.

Without a subscription you can keep 1 tank, 7 days of history, and 2 reminders. Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription that unlocks unlimited tanks, history, and reminders, plus charts, custom ranges, a livestock list, CSV export, and a home screen widget. Subscriptions are offered monthly and yearly through Apple. The monthly plan includes a 1-week free introductory offer for eligible users. The yearly plan has no free trial.
