Apps · Aquarium Tracker: Brineleaf

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Brineleaf (the "app," listed on the App Store as "Aquarium Tracker: Brineleaf") is made by John Gillespie, an independent developer ("I," "me"). This policy explains, in plain English, what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect your personal data. Everything it uses stays on your iPhone.

The short version

  • Tanks, water tests, care schedules, and app settings stay on your iPhone. They are stored on the device with SwiftData.
  • Care reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your iPhone only.
  • Charts, custom ranges, the livestock list, and the home screen widget, when you use them, stay on your device.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no data is collected by me.
  • Nothing is sent to any server by me.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. I never see your payment card details.
  • I do not sell your data. I don't have any to sell.

Information stored on your device

Your tanks, water-test logs, care schedule, reminders, and app settings are stored locally on your iPhone with SwiftData. A water-test log can include pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, and temperature, and optionally GH, KH, and salinity. The care schedule can include water changes, filter cleaning, and feeding. If you use Premium, the livestock list and custom ranges are stored the same way. I don't operate any servers, and this information is not sent to me or to anyone else.

Charts are drawn on your iPhone from the test logs stored on the device.

If your iPhone's iCloud device backup (or another device backup) is turned on, Apple may include this on-device data in that backup, according to your backup settings. I don't receive that backup.

Care reminders

The app can schedule local notifications to remind you about tank care, such as a water change, filter cleaning, or feeding. Those notifications are scheduled on your iPhone only. They are not sent through a server I run. You can turn notifications on or off anytime in Settings > Notifications.

Home screen widget

The home screen widget (Premium) reads the app's data on your device. That data stays on your iPhone. It is not sent to me or to any server I run.

CSV export

Premium can export your logs as a CSV file on your iPhone. That file is created on your device. I don't receive it. If you save it or share it with another app, that copy is handled by you and by that app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment, and I never receive your payment card information. The app checks with Apple (using Apple's StoreKit) to know whether you have an active subscription. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

The app has no sign-up or login. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. It does not track you across other apps or websites. Nothing in the app is sent to any server by me. I do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal data, there is nothing to collect from a child either. If you have questions, contact me using the details below.

Your choices and deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your iPhone, you're in control:

  • Turn off notifications anytime in Settings > Notifications.
  • Delete your data by deleting the app. Removing Brineleaf from your iPhone deletes your tanks, water tests, care schedule, reminders, livestock list, and settings stored on the device, including data the widget reads. (If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, copies may remain in those backups until they're replaced or deleted under your backup settings.)
  • Cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

California residents

Brineleaf does not collect, sell, or share personal information as those terms are used in California privacy law. If you have a question about your privacy rights, contact me below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, such as the app starting to collect new information, I'll update this policy before the change takes effect.
