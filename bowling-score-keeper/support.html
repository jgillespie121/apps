FAQ

How do I restore purchases?

Open Bowling Score Keeper and use Restore Purchases in the app. Restoring asks Apple to re-check your Apple ID for an active subscription for Keptpin (Bowling Score Keeper). Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Where are my scores and stats stored?

On your iPhone. Bowling Score Keeper stores games, scores, and stats on the device with SwiftData. There is no account, and nothing is sent to a server run by the developer. If iCloud or another device backup is on, Apple may include that on-device data in the backup under your backup settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings. On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apple ID (your name) > Subscriptions, select Keptpin (Bowling Score Keeper), and cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. You keep your subscription until the period you've already paid for ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What are the subscription plans?

The app offers auto-renewing monthly and yearly subscriptions through Apple. The monthly plan includes a 1-week free introductory offer for eligible users. The yearly plan does not include a free trial. The price of each plan is shown in the App Store and may vary by region. Subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. See the Terms of Use for full details.

Is my data private?

Yes. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Games, scores, and stats stay on your phone. No data is collected by the developer. See the Privacy Policy for the full story.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes games, scores, stats, and settings stored on the device. If iCloud or another device backup is on, copies may remain in that backup until you delete them under your backup settings. Cancel your subscription separately in Apple ID settings if you no longer want to be billed.
